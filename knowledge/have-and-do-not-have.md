# What we have and what we do not have

Step 1 of the guide, product knowledge. This is the file that stops the AI agent from inventing a feature. Write it as two plain lists. If a lead asks about something that is in neither list, the AI agent does not guess: the question goes to a person, and the answer goes into one of the lists.

Source: (product pages, your product team, your sales rep)
Last checked: (YYYY-MM-DD, and who checked)

## We have

Features, integrations and services the product really has today. One line each, in plain words. Write the plan each one belongs to if it is not in every plan.

## We have in part

Things that exist but with limits. State the limit exactly, so the AI agent does not round it up.

<!-- example: An integration, how it works, and what it does not do. -->

-

## We do not have

Things leads ask for that the product does not do. Every line here is a ban on a promise. List everything your sales rep has had to say "no" to in the last months, even if it looks obvious.

<!-- example: Something you do not have, in one line. -->

-

## On the roadmap

Only what you are willing to say in public, with the wording you want used. If there is nothing you are willing to say, leave this empty. The AI agent never promises a date and never says "soon".

<!-- example: Something planned. Write it only if a person may promise it, and never give a date. If you share no plans, write: "We do not share plans or dates for future features." -->

-

## What to say when the answer is no

One short, honest line for each important "we do not have", plus what you can offer instead. A "no" that ends with a useful next step keeps the lead.
