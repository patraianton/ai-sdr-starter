# Objections and their answers

Step 1 of the guide, product knowledge. The objections your sales rep hears most, each with the answer that works. Take them from your SDR's threads and from call notes. For each objection write the lead's typical words, the answer, and the next step the answer ends with.

Source: (the threads and call notes these come from)
Last checked: (YYYY-MM-DD)

Rules for the answers:

- An answer states facts from this folder only. No new numbers, no new promises.
- An answer is short. If it needs more than five sentences, it is a call, not an email.
- An answer ends with the next step: a question, a link, or the booking link.
- A clear refusal is not an objection. "No, stop writing" goes to `rules/01-what-to-answer.md`, not here.
