# Launch rules

Step 1 of the guide, rule file 7 of 8. Setup 1 only: Northstar runs outbound through Apollo and Smartlead.

## Who pays
- Every quarter, export the HubSpot customers with size, industry, country and accounting system. The segments where the revenue sits are the ICP.
- Do not widen the ICP because small companies reply readily. Example: a company with nine technicians replied warmly in March and would pay for Crew only.

## Apollo filters
- Industries: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair. Countries: United States, Canada.
- Titles: Owner, General Manager, Operations Manager, Dispatch Manager.
- Employees: 20 to 400, a wide net. The company lookup in 02-what-to-check.md sets the real size, and the floor of 15 technicians decides.

## Suppression lists
- Before the first send, load into Smartlead: every HubSpot customer, every company with an open deal, every domain Sam or Dana has ever written to, and everyone who said no or unsubscribed.
- Reload the lists every Monday before new contacts go in. Sam does it.

## Volume
- 150 new contacts per week. 25 emails per mailbox per day. Verify every address in ZeroBounce first and load only the valid ones.

## The bounce rate that pauses the campaign
- At a bounce rate of 3 percent, pause the campaign in Smartlead and tell Sam in the same run. Priya decides whether it restarts.
