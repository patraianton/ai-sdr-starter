# What to answer and what to skip

Step 1 of the guide, rule file 1 of 8. Northstar Dispatch, setups 1 and 3.

## Read the whole thread
- Read the whole thread and the card first, never the last message alone.
- Example: Pete Ostrander writes "Can we talk?" under three earlier emails. Find the one-pager he read and the question he asked, then answer that.

## Answer these
- A question about the product or the price, such as "Does it sync with QuickBooks?" Answer from knowledge/ in plain words and end with a question or the booking link.
- An objection, such as "We already run dispatch from a spreadsheet." Use the matching answer in knowledge/objections.md, then ask what breaks in the spreadsheet today.
- A positive reply, such as "Sounds interesting, tell me more." Answer in two sentences, then ask the first open question from 03-icp-and-qualification.md.
- A fact about their setup, such as "We have 40 technicians on QuickBooks Online." Write it on the card as found-out before you write the reply.
- A question that knowledge/ does not answer, such as "Is there an app for technicians?" Do not answer it. Put it on the waiting list for Sam (08-approval-and-people.md).

## Skip these
- Out-of-office replies and other autoresponders. Write the reply note on the card, send nothing, and let the follow-up schedule run.
- "Unsubscribe", "remove me" or "stop emailing". Label the card No fit with the reason "refused", close it, stop the lead's sequence in Smartlead, add the address to the suppression list, send nothing.
- A clear refusal, such as "Not interested" or "We signed with someone else" with no end date. Same as above. No thank-you email.

## Park these
- "Not now, write me in June." Label the card Parked with the date 2026-06-01, send one short email that confirms the date, and come back on it.
- "We signed with someone else and the contract ends in May." That is a "not now" with a date. Park the card on the first working day after the end date.
- "Not now" with no date. Ask once: "Which month should I write to you again?" If there is no answer in 7 days, park the card for 90 days.
