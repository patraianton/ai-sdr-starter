# Follow-ups and when to stop

Step 1 of the guide, rule file 5 of 8. Northstar Dispatch. The numbers live in settings.yml: 3 unanswered emails in a row, and the first email counts. That is the first email and 2 follow-ups, on days 3 and 7 after the previous email, Monday to Friday.

## What does not stop follow-ups
- An out-of-office reply or another autoresponder. The sequence runs.
- A reply alone. After the lead replies and the AI agent answers, the count starts again from that answer.

## What stops them
- A booking or a trial sign-up. Label the card Booked, or write a handover to Dana.
- An explicit no, a no-show, or 3 emails in a row with no answer.
- Our person writing in the thread (02-what-to-check.md), or the label "Do not write".

## What each follow-up adds
- One new thing each time: a case study or one-pager that matches their industry or system, an answer to a question they have not asked yet, or a fact from the card.
- Never "just checking in", "bumping this" or "any thoughts?".
- Example: Maple Electric asked about electricians, so its second email adds route optimization from Fleet.
- When a short link is opened for the first time, write the next message in that run, not on the schedule. If our last email is under 24 hours old, wait for the lead's answer instead.

## A lead who goes quiet
- After the third email with no answer, write a `next-step` note: "Do: nothing, the sequence ended after 3 unanswered emails", with "On: none". Keep the label and do not close the card. The AI agent skips the card until the lead replies.
