# Who fits and what to ask

Step 1 of the guide, rule file 3 of 8. Northstar Dispatch. A lead that fits is worth a call. A lead that does not fit gets a short, honest answer.

## Industry
- HVAC, plumbing, electrical and appliance repair. Field-service companies only.

## Country
- United States and Canada. A lead from any other country gets the label No fit.

## Size floor
- Size is the number of technicians. The floor of 15 is size_floor in settings.yml. A good fit has 15 to 300 technicians.
- Below the floor but wrote to us: no call and no follow-ups. Send one reply with the public pricing page and the 14-day trial, then close the card as No fit.

## What must already be in place
- The company already uses a CRM or an accounting system, such as HubSpot, Pipedrive or QuickBooks Online. Ask if Apollo and the website do not show it.

## Hard disqualifiers
- One-truck operators and any company below the size floor.
- Franchisors that resell software, and software vendors that compete with us.
- Companies outside the US and Canada.

## Questions to get answered
- Fit: how many technicians do you have, and which CRM or accounting system do you use?
- A real task: what are you trying to fix, the dispatch board, routes, billing or overtime?
- The person who decides: who else would join the call, and who signs?
- "Call me" and "send it over" are not qualified leads. Answer in two sentences, ask what is still open, and send the booking link only when all three are answered. Write the answers on the card in a found-out note.
