# Questions leads ask

Step 1 of the guide, product knowledge. Each answer is short enough to paste into an email. If a question is not here, it goes to Sam, and a person adds his answer here the same day.

Source: the March 2026 threads on the cards in examples/northstar/cards/, and the public price page.
Last checked: 2026-03-02, Sam Okafor

## How it works
- **What does Northstar Dispatch do?** It is scheduling and dispatch software for field-service companies: HVAC, plumbing, electrical and appliance repair, in the US and Canada.
- **Do you have route optimization?** Yes, in the Fleet plan.
- **Can I talk to someone?** Yes. Dana Reyes hosts an intro call: https://calendly.com/northstar-dispatch/intro-call

## Integrations
- **Does it work with QuickBooks?** Yes, with QuickBooks Online. The sync is part of the Fleet plan.
- **Does it work with QuickBooks Desktop?** It is not on our list of integrations. The question goes to Sam before anyone answers it.
- **Does it sync with Xero?** No. We do not have a Xero sync.
- **Which other tools does it connect to?** Google Calendar, Twilio SMS and Zapier, and QuickBooks Online on Fleet. Which plans include the first three is not in these files, so that question goes to Sam.
- **Can my customers book through an app?** No. We do not have a mobile app for customers.
- **Does it work offline?** No. We do not have an offline mode.
- **Does it run payroll or track technician pay?** No. We do not have payroll.
- **Does it manage parts and inventory?** No. We do not have inventory management.

## Pricing and trial
- **How much does it cost?** Crew is $59 per dispatcher per month, Fleet is $149 per dispatcher per month, and Enterprise is by quote. Prices are on https://northstardispatch.example/pricing.
- **Is there a free trial?** Yes. It runs 14 days and needs no card.
- **How many technicians can I have?** Crew covers up to 10 technicians. Fleet has no limit on technicians.

## Security and data
- **Do you offer SSO or an API?** Yes, in Enterprise.
- **Where is our data kept, and can we export it?** Not in these files. The question goes to Sam.
