# Dispatch with QuickBooks Online

Step 1 of the guide. Published at https://northstardispatch.example/one-pagers/quickbooks-sync (index id: op-quickbooks-sync).

## What it is
QuickBooks sync connects Northstar Dispatch to QuickBooks Online. It is part of the Fleet plan.

## Who it is for
Companies that dispatch from Northstar Dispatch and keep their books in QuickBooks Online.

## The plan
Fleet is $149 per dispatcher per month, with unlimited technicians and route optimization. Full prices: https://northstardispatch.example/pricing

## Good to know
- We sync with QuickBooks Online. QuickBooks Desktop is not on our list of integrations.
- We do not have a Xero sync.
- The trial is 14 days and needs no card.
