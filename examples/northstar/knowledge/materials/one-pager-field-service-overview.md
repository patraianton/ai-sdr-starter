# Northstar Dispatch for field-service companies

Step 1 of the guide. Published at https://northstardispatch.example/one-pagers/field-service-overview (index id: op-field-service-overview).

## What it is
Scheduling and dispatch software for HVAC, plumbing, electrical and appliance repair companies in the US and Canada.

## Plans
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Crew | $59 per dispatcher per month | Up to 10 technicians |
| Fleet | $149 per dispatcher per month | Unlimited technicians, route optimization, QuickBooks sync |
| Enterprise | By quote | SSO, API, dedicated onboarding |

## Connects to
QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar, Twilio SMS and Zapier.

## Try it
The trial is 14 days and needs no card. Prices: https://northstardispatch.example/pricing

## What we do not have
Inventory management, payroll, a mobile app for customers, a Xero sync and an offline mode.
