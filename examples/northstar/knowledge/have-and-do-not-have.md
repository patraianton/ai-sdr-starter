# What we have and what we do not have

Step 1 of the guide, product knowledge. This is the list the AI agent checks before it writes any sentence about the product. If a feature is not on the first list, do not write it.

Source: the public price page and Sam Okafor.
Last checked: 2026-03-02, Sam Okafor

## We have
- Scheduling and dispatch software for field-service companies in the US and Canada.
- Plans: Crew (up to 10 technicians), Fleet (unlimited technicians), Enterprise (by quote).
- Route optimization, in Fleet.
- QuickBooks sync with QuickBooks Online, in Fleet.
- Integrations: QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar, Twilio SMS and Zapier.
- SSO, an API and dedicated onboarding, in Enterprise.
- A 14-day trial with no card.

## We have in part
- Google Calendar, Twilio SMS and Zapier are listed as integrations. Which plans include them is not in these files, so do not say.

## We do not have
- Inventory management.
- Payroll.
- A mobile app for customers.
- A Xero sync.
- Offline mode.

## On the roadmap
- We do not share plans or dates for future features. Never say "soon".

## What to say when the answer is no
- Xero: "We do not have a Xero sync. We sync with QuickBooks Online. Which one do you use?"
- Mobile app for customers: "We do not have one. What would you want your customers to do on their phones? That tells me whether a call helps."
- Inventory, payroll or offline mode: "We do not have that. We do scheduling and dispatch. What would you want fixed first?"
- Anything not named above, such as QuickBooks Desktop, an app for technicians, uptime figures or security certificates: do not answer. Put the question on the waiting list for Sam.
