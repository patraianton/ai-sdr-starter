# Objections and answers

Step 1 of the guide, product knowledge. These are the objections Dana hears most. Each answer is the one to give, and each ends with a question that moves the lead to the next step. Never answer with a feature that is not in have-and-do-not-have.md.

Source: the March 2026 threads on the example cards, and Dana's call notes.
Last checked: 2026-03-02, Dana Reyes

## Too expensive
- **"It is too expensive."** The price is per dispatcher, not per technician: Crew $59, Fleet $149 per month. Ask how many dispatchers they have, then give the per-dispatcher price of the plan that fits. Never add up a total.

## We already have a tool
- **"We already run dispatch from a spreadsheet and it works."** Agree that it works. Ask what breaks on a bad day: a job that runs long, a technician who calls in, a rush call. Offer the 14-day trial, which needs no card.
- **"We already use other dispatch software."** Do not compare features. Ask what they would change about it. If the answer is a feature we list, name the plan. If not, offer the call.
- **"We need QuickBooks Desktop or Xero."** Say it plainly. We sync with QuickBooks Online, on Fleet. We do not have a Xero sync. For QuickBooks Desktop, the question goes to Sam.

## Not now
- **"We just signed a contract with someone else."** Ask when the contract ends. With a date, park the card on the first working day after it (01-what-to-answer.md). Without a date, it is a refusal.

## Send me information
- **"Send me some information."** Send the one-pager that matches their industry or system as a short link, then ask the first open question from the qualification rules.

## I need to ask my team
- **"I need my boss to approve this."** Ask who decides and offer to include that person in the call with Dana.

## Switching is too much work
- **"Our technicians will not use new software."** Do not promise adoption. Say the trial is 14 days with no card, so one dispatcher can test it first. Ask who would test it.

## Trust and security
- **"Do you have SSO or strong security?"** SSO is in Enterprise. Anything else about security is not in these files, so it goes to Sam.

## An objection that is new
- Not in these files yet: a competitor's free plan. The AI agent hands it to Sam on the card. Sam's answer is added here under a new heading the same day.
