# Plans and prices

Step 1 of the guide, product knowledge. Northstar Dispatch, exactly as on the public price page.

Source: https://northstardispatch.example/pricing. If this file and the page differ, the page wins and Sam fixes this file the same day.
Last checked: 2026-03-02, Sam Okafor

## Plans
- Crew: $59 per dispatcher per month. Up to 10 technicians.
- Fleet: $149 per dispatcher per month. Unlimited technicians, route optimization, QuickBooks sync.
- Enterprise: by quote. SSO, API, dedicated onboarding.

## Billing
- The price is per dispatcher per month, in US dollars.
- Annual contracts, taxes and payment terms are not on the page. The AI agent does not answer them. The question goes to Sam.

## Trial
- 14 days. No card needed.
- What happens when the trial ends is not on the page. The question goes to Sam.

## Add-ons and limits
- Crew stops at 10 technicians. Fleet has no limit on technicians.
- QuickBooks sync and route optimization are listed under Fleet only. Crew does not list them.
- SSO, the API and dedicated onboarding are listed under Enterprise only.
- No add-ons are listed on the page.

## What the AI agent does with a price question
- Send the price page link and the one line for the plan that fits the lead's technician count. The price is per dispatcher, so ask how many dispatchers there are.
- Never calculate a custom total or a discount.

## Discounts
- No discounts. Every discount request goes to Sam.

## Prices the AI agent must never state
- Enterprise: never name a number. Say it is by quote and offer the call with Dana.
- Implementation fees, partner rates and past prices. They are not on the page.
